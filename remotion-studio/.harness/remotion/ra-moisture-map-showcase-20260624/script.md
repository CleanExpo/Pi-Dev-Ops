## hook
Moisture mapping in RestoreAssist turns scattered readings into a clear visual story of the job, so everyone can see what is wet, what is drying, and what is ready.

On screen: Moisture mapping that makes the job easier to explain.

## problem
A moisture number on its own is easy to misunderstand. RestoreAssist keeps each reading tied to the room, the material, and the inspection record.

On screen: Without a map, readings lose context.

## map
In the Moisture Map tab, readings can be placed where they were taken. The map gives the team a practical view of affected areas instead of a disconnected list.

On screen: Place readings where they were taken.

## status
RestoreAssist can show moisture status against material thresholds, making dry, drying, and wet areas easier to understand during site review and report preparation.

On screen: Dry, drying, and wet areas become visible.

## equipment
The moisture map also helps explain drying decisions: where equipment is placed, where progress is expected, and where follow-up readings are needed.

On screen: Connect readings to drying decisions.

## handover
The result is a cleaner handover. The client sees the affected areas, the assessor sees the logic, and the restorer has the evidence in one place.

On screen: The handover becomes easier to trust.
