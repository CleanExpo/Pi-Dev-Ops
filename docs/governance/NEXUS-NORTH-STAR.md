# Unite-Group Nexus North Star — Built for the Hard Day

**Founder approved:** 29 September 2026

**Owner:** Phill McGurk

**Scope:** Unite-Group purpose and conduct across the portfolio. This is a vision and decision lens; it does not replace the constitution, decision rights, release gates, or product-specific missions.

> Build a group of businesses, people and systems that earn trust when conditions are hardest.

Nexus turns ambition into responsible action. It protects the mission, helps people tell the truth early, gives the founder clear choices, and keeps evidence and human care visible alongside revenue and delivery.

The group exists to improve outcomes for restoration businesses, technicians and people affected by damage. Financial strength sustains that work; it is not the whole definition of success.

## Our promises

| To | Promise | Proof in the work |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Customers | Tell the truth about the job, protect their information and remain accountable when it gets difficult. | Honest status, clear ownership, timely correction and verified outcomes. |
| Team | Make it safe to raise bad news early, offer useful challenge and give people a fair path to repair mistakes and grow. | Early escalation, two-way feedback and an incident review that produces a checked repair. |
| Partners | Make commitments we can keep, record material terms and act with integrity when interests diverge. | References, written agreements, bounded trials and observable conduct under pressure. |
| Founder and Board | Show reality clearly, including uncertainty and dissent. | A decision card with evidence, options, consequences, authority boundary and next check. |

## Operating principles

1. **Choose for the hard day.** Select partners and senior people on references, past conduct, clear agreements and a small real collaboration. Apply the standard to ourselves. Do not secretly test people through personal hardship.
2. **Trust is earned in action.** Look for promises kept, problems raised early, repair after mistakes and respect in disagreement. Never automate a person's worth or loyalty score.
3. **Tell the truth while it can help.** Flag a failing test, cash risk, unhappy customer or weak assumption in time to act. A green status needs current evidence; missing evidence reads unknown.
4. **Give the founder useful challenge.** Present evidence and dissent plainly. The Board, judge and approval gates remain intact; the system does not approve its own work.
5. **Protect focus.** Show a small set of active priorities. Park ideas until mission fit, capacity, dependencies, cost and customer value are checked.
6. **Grow people as the system grows.** Give clear ownership, timely feedback, room to learn and support under pressure. Sustainable work helps the long-term mission.
7. **Recover visibly.** Contain harm, state what is known and unknown, name an owner, choose the next safe step, verify the repair and record the lesson.

## Required expression in the build

- **Mission Control** answers: Where are we? What matters now? What is hard? What needs Phill's decision? What was truly shipped? Each answer links to current evidence or says unknown.
- **`/spm`** resumes authorised work and returns those answers. Its decision card names impact, evidence, options, dissent, owner, authority boundary and next check.
- **Product and release work** shows how a proposal improves the customer or team promise. A PR, merge or green test is not by itself proof that customers have a working release.
- **Hiring and partners** use fair, documented checks of conduct and fit. No private relationship, health or loyalty scoring.
- **Incident reviews** measure detection, escalation, verified recovery and recurrence. Learning does not excuse avoidable harm.
