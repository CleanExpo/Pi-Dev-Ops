## 3. Decisions Locked + What Shipped

- **Diagnosis that drove the build:** bewg.au serves byte-identical content at `/`, `/services` and
  `/contact` — a single page for a firm offering eight investigation types. Verified 3 Sep by
  fetching all three. Every benchmark Ivi supplied (Element FE, PCT Building Science, Sarveli, Makao)
  is structured around individually addressable service pages.
- **Decision: content-driven, not hand-authored HTML.** All copy in `content/*.json`; templates
  generate pages. Adding a service is one JSON entry.
- **Decision: build fails closed on content defects** — dead `related` link, empty required field,
  triage answer pointing at a missing service, or a service no triage answer routes to.
- **Decision: no backend.** The finder's brief goes out through the visitor's own mail client
  (`mailto:`). Nothing posted, nothing stored, no PII held. A form endpoint is a deliberate
  next step, not an oversight.
- **Decision: copy claims method and standards, never credentials.** Unverified claims are listed
  in the site README for Ivi to confirm before publication.
- Shipped: `origin/claude/ecstatic-newton-qnmzzg` at `100b19d`.
