# Viral playbook — the short-form shot grammar

The hero short is built to survive three attention gates: the thumb-stop (frame 1),
the hook (second 1–2), and the mid-drop (second 3–5). Everything below serves those.

## The 7-second spine

- **0.0–0.5s · Pattern interrupt.** First frame must stop the scroll on its own — motion,
  a face mid-expression, an on-screen claim, or a visual anomaly. No logos, no slow fades,
  no title cards. If the first frame works as a thumbnail, it works as a stopper.
- **0.5–2.0s · Hook.** The single promise or tension, spoken and burned on screen
  (nexus-copywriter supplies both). One idea. Curiosity gap or stakes, not a summary.
- **2.0–5.0s · Retention beats.** Three quick beats that each pay a little and owe a little —
  proof, twist, then the turn. No dead middle; cut on motion, never on silence.
- **5.0–7.0s · Loop-back close.** Resolve the hook and seed a reason to rewatch or comment.
  The last frame should hand back to the first (clean loop) where the format allows.

## Retention shape

Aim for a rising-then-held curve, not a slow build. The failure mode is the "dead middle" —
seconds 3–5 with no new information. Every beat introduces one new thing (a fact, a face,
a cut, a caption reveal). If a beat only restates, cut it.

## 9:16 craft rules

- Safe zone: keep faces and key captions inside the centre 80% vertically; platform UI
  (captions, profile, buttons) eats the bottom ~15% and right ~12%.
- Captions burned in, high-contrast, ≤2 lines, legible fully muted — the majority watch
  sound-off. Caption timing leads the audio by ~80ms so it never lags the mouth.
- One focal subject per frame. Vertical crop kills wide compositions — shoot/generate tight.
- Motion every ~1.5s (cut, push-in, or caption reveal) to hold the scroll reflex.

## Model routing for shorts

Route through the Synthex registry, draft-first. Human-face talking shorts should avoid
models that reject real faces (e.g. Seedance) and prefer face-safe hosted models; prefer
pre-paid Artlist over metered fal for the hosted tier. Keep the hero ≤8s per clip and
stitch beats rather than generating one long take — cheaper, and it enforces the cut cadence.

## What does NOT go in a short

No slow intros, no "hey guys welcome back", no logo stings before the hook, no more than
one CTA, no unmuted-only payoff (the joke/point must survive with sound off).
