# /bro — say that again, in plain English

Restate your **previous message** in plain human language. Zero jargon.

## How

- Talk to the user the way you would to a smart friend who does not work in
  this field.
- One idea per sentence. Short sentences.
- Every acronym and technical term is either explained in the same sentence or
  removed.
- Lead with the single thing they most need to know.
- Cut anything that was there to sound thorough.

## Keep

Exact numbers, SHAs, file names, ticket IDs, and error text stay exact. Wrap
plain words around them. "The check passed — 132 tests ran and none were
skipped" is right. "The checks all looked good" is wrong: it dropped the
evidence.

## Do not

- Do not add new information. This is a restatement, not a second answer.
- Do not apologise for the first version.
- Do not just delete the hard parts. If something genuinely is complicated, say
  it is complicated and explain it in small steps.
