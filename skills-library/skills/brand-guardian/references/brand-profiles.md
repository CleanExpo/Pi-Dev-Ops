# Business Brand Profiles

Quick reference — always defer to the charter file (`~/.hermes/business-charters/[brand].md`) if it exists.

**DR (Disaster Recovery)**
- Voice: direct, contractor-led, no managed repair spin, anti-bureaucracy
- Audience: ANZ trade contractors, restorers, assessors
- Forbidden: "seamless", "solution provider", anything that sounds like a managed repairer

**NRPG (National Restoration Practitioners Group)**
- Voice: authority, peer-to-peer, industry-first, community-led
- Audience: ANZ restoration industry professionals
- Forbidden: corporate language, anything that sounds like insurer-speak

**RestoreAssist**
- Voice: Klark Brown construct — see [[voice-klark-brown]]
- Audience: trade contractors using iOS
- Forbidden: consumer-app language, overpromising on AI accuracy

**CARSI**
- Voice: compliance-confident, precise, no ambiguity
- Audience: compliance managers, insurers, legal teams
- Forbidden: casual language, hedging on regulatory claims

**CCW (Carpet Cleaners Warehouse)**
- Voice: trade-direct, product-knowledgeable, no retail fluff
- Audience: professional carpet cleaners, commercial operators
- Forbidden: consumer-brand warmth, aspirational lifestyle language

**Synthex**
- Voice: technical authority, automation-focused, ROI-grounded
- Audience: marketing ops teams, agency operators, SaaS buyers
- Forbidden: hype language, unquantified claims ("10x your results")

**Unite-Group (parent)**
- Voice: founder-led, empire-building, long-term thinking
- Forbidden: startup-speak, "disruption", "unicorn", VC-culture language
