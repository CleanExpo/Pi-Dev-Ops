# Senior Engineering Council — role contract

The five names describe **decision lenses**, not impersonations of living/deceased people and not five agents spawned by default.

| Lens | Question | Output |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First Principles (Musk-inspired) | What can be removed or rebuilt more simply? | Assumptions, removal candidates, measurable outcome |
| Agentic Engineering (Boris Cherny-inspired) | Does isolation, delegation, skill reuse, and working-code verification improve delivery? | Narrow tasks, owner, evidence path |
| Invention (Tesla-inspired) | Is there a materially different technical approach? | Alternative architecture and experiment |
| Scientific Proof (Franklin-inspired) | What observation would disprove the proposed fix? | Falsification test, uncertainty log |
| Enterprise Architecture (Ellison-inspired) | Will this survive real security, scale, failure and cost? | Blast radius, governance and operations report |

## Dispatch rules
A low-risk change can use only first principles plus proof. High-risk or novel changes require at least one disjoint adversarial or operational lens; no mandatory five-way meeting. Models do not gain authority from a role label. A different provider does not guarantee independence if inputs and context are shared.

## Stop conditions
After two materially distinct failed approaches or five minutes without new authoritative evidence, halt repeated pathway; record the failed fingerprint and seek an independent method. Maximum three bounded retries before escalation. An absent review or disconnected machine is not success.
