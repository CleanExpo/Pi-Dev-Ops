# Advisory-board composition contract

Use this only after a confirmed, sealed Grill shared-understanding receipt. It composes five AI
advisory personas around one decision. It does not select people, hire anyone, impersonate public
figures, diagnose personality, or activate agents.

## Preconditions

- Bind the Grill objective, sketch, decision-tree, transcript, and receipt digests.
- Keep secrets, credentials, raw client material, real-person profiles, and psychometric profiles
  out of the board input.
- Set a data class, allowed execution location, finite cost cap, and deadline.
- Client or confidential work stays local-only unless a separate trusted policy permits otherwise.

## Personas and protocol

The fixed functional personas are outcome, systems, customer value, risk, and economics. For each
role, discovery produces at least two **functional candidate profiles**, then ranks them by mandate
fit, capability evidence, complementarity, counter-argument quality, and known limits. The selected
profile remains a proposal: it is not a claim about, impersonation of, or assessment of a real
person. First passes are independent. Each claim is `FACT`, `INFERENCE`, `ASSUMPTION`, or `UNKNOWN`;
facts cite an evidence ID. The claim ledger is anonymised before one bounded cross-examination round.

Before dispatch, one route manifest binds every persona and arbiter to provider family, resolved
model, endpoint, execution location, provider-registry ID, policy digest, and a finite reservation.
Every resolved lane then records that exact route digest, the observed endpoint/location/model,
price snapshot digest, observed cost, and bounded token usage. A valid run has at least two provider
families and no reused resolved model.
An independent top-floor arbiter receives the evidence ledger, score matrix, and dissent, then may
return only `APPROVE_FOR_NEXT_GATE`, `DEFERRED_NEEDS_EVIDENCE`, or `NO_GO`.

## Gates

- No silent fallback or downgrade of privacy, capability, or verifier independence: observed route
  values must exactly match the pre-dispatch route manifest.
- Missing usage, a cost-cap breach, unsourced facts, or a critical unresolved risk is blocked.
- An arbiter cannot share a provider family with persona authors and cannot execute work.
- A result remains `proposal_only` until a separate independent verifier and explicit owner approval
  through a trusted authority runtime. No board output auto-activates agents or workers.

This is a custom capability and evidence matrix. It is not a Belbin Team Roles assessment or Hogan
Leadership Forecast assessment; those instruments and their related reports must not be reproduced,
modified, or claimed by this skill.
